NPL 2026 AI Overview & Prediction

Nepal Premier League Season 3 · 26 Oct – 21 Nov 2026 · Last updated 11 October 2026

Before the third season starts, here is everything the numbers say about the Nepal Premier League: how the Tribhuvan University ground in Kirtipur plays, how each of the eight teams has performed, and how the opening opponents have fared against each other. The stats cover the 64 NPL matches played in 2024 and 2025, all at TU Cricket Ground.

Key takeaways

  • Par score is about 150. The average first innings total at TU is 148.
  • Chasing has a small edge: 34 of 64 matches (53.1%) were won by the team batting second.
  • Scoring swings widely: from 51 all out to 220/6 in the two seasons so far.
  • Finishing first has not won titles. Sudurpaschim Royals topped the table both years and lost both finals. The 2nd-placed team won in 2024 and the 4th-placed team won in 2025.
  • Sudurpaschim Royals are the most consistent side with 14 wins in 18 matches, but still no trophy.

What this means for NPL 2026

  • With an average first innings of 148, a total of around 150 has been a competitive score here, and scores above 180 have been rare.
  • The 53% to 47% split is small over 64 matches, so the toss and chase decision are not decisive on their own.
  • The second-innings average of 132 is lower partly because successful chases finish early, before all 20 overs are used.
  • Big totals are possible: Biratnagar Kings hit 220/6 in 2025, and in the 2024 final Janakpur Bolts chased down 185 in 19.2 overs.
