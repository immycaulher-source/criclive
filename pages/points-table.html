How the points system works

The NPL uses the standard T20 league points system. These rules follow the format used in earlier NPL seasons, so check the official playing conditions for any change this year.

Win2points
Tie / No result1point each
Loss0points

Each of the eight teams plays the other seven teams once, so a team can earn a maximum of 14 points in the league stage. Teams are ranked by points first. If two or more teams finish level on points, net run rate (NRR) decides who ranks higher.

What is net run rate?

NRR is the average number of runs a team scores per over minus the average number of runs it concedes per over, across the whole tournament. For example, if a team scores 180 in 20 overs (9.00 an over) and the opponent scores 160 in 20 overs (8.00 an over), that match adds +1.000 to the winner's NRR. A higher NRR means a team has been winning by bigger margins or chasing down targets faster.

How teams reach the playoffs

After the 28 league matches, the top four teams qualify. Playoff matches do not add points to the table.

17 Nov
Qualifier 1: 1st place vs 2nd place
18 Nov
Eliminator: 3rd place vs 4th place
19 Nov
Qualifier 2: loser of Qualifier 1 vs winner of the Eliminator
21 Nov
Grand Finale: winner of Qualifier 1 vs winner of Qualifier 2

Finishing in the top two gives a team two chances to reach the final, which is why every league point and a good NRR matter.

Frequently asked questions

How many points does a team get for a win?

Two points for a win and none for a loss. A tie or a no result gives one point to each team.

What if teams are level on points?

Net run rate separates them: the team with the higher NRR is ranked higher.

How many teams make the playoffs?

The top four of the eight teams qualify after the 28 league matches.

When is the Grand Finale?

On Saturday 21 November 2026 at the TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur.
