Frequently asked questions

How many matches are there in NPL 2026?

There are 32 matches: 28 league matches (each team plays seven) and four playoff matches, including the Grand Finale on 21 November 2026.

When does NPL 2026 start and end?

It starts on 26 October 2026 with Lumbini Lions vs Sudurpaschim Royals and ends with the Grand Finale on 21 November.

What time do the matches start?

4:30 PM Nepal time on single-match days. On double-header days, 12:30 PM and 4:30 PM.

Where are the matches played?

All matches are at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu.

How do the playoffs work?

The top four teams qualify. Qualifier 1 is 1st vs 2nd and the Eliminator is 3rd vs 4th. The loser of Qualifier 1 plays the winner of the Eliminator in Qualifier 2, and the Qualifier 1 winner and Qualifier 2 winner meet in the Grand Finale.
