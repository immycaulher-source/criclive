Nepal Premier League Season 3 · TU Ground, Kirtipur · Last updated 5 October 2026

Nothing beats watching the Nepal Premier League from the stands at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground. Who sells NPL 2026 tickets?

When CAN opened the media and ticketing bids on 7 September 2026, eSewa Ltd. won the ticketing rights with a bid of NPR 85 lakh. Under the agreed terms CAN keeps 95 percent of the ticketing income and eSewa receives 5 percent. This means tickets should be bought through eSewa or other channels that CAN officially announces. Treat any other seller with caution.

How to buy NPL tickets

  1. Open the eSewa app, or use the NPL ticket page linked above. Create or sign in to your eSewa account.
  2. Choose the match you want and the seat category.
  3. Select the number of seats and pay using the payment options eSewa offers.
  4. Keep your confirmation and e-ticket safe. You will normally need it to enter the stadium.

The exact screens may differ slightly from these steps as eSewa updates its app.

Match day tips

  • Arrive early. Kirtipur gets busy on match days, and entry queues can be long, especially for the opening games.
  • Plan your travel. Public transport or a shared ride is usually easier than finding parking near the ground.
  • Carry your ticket and phone charged. Digital tickets are only useful if your battery lasts.
  • Check entry rules. Items allowed inside are set by CAN and the stadium. Check their announcements before you pack a bag.
  • Dress for the weather. Late October and November days in Kathmandu are usually sunny, with cool evenings.

Avoid fake tickets

  • Buy only from eSewa or channels officially announced by CAN or the NPL.
  • Do not buy from strangers on social media, and never share the QR code or booking code from your ticket.
  • If a deal looks too good to be true, or a seller asks for payment to a personal account, walk away.
